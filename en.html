This project served as an introduction to isometric illustration. The task was to visualize my bedroom using isometric projections, with a strict one-day deadline. Word mapping [isometry]

More information

Excited to dive back into isometric work, I used this second project to broaden my technical skills. I spent time testing different workflows and effects before finalizing my 'word-image' composition. The goal was to ensure the illustration captured and visualized the essence of the chosen word through an isometric perspective.

Snakes and Ladders [Isometrie]

More information

This project followed a complete design workflow, starting with initial analog sketches and moving into isometric digital illustration. To give the project an energetic and modern feel, I opted for a palette of bright, high-contrast colors

Bird illustration

More information

As a final 'challenge' to wrap up my first year of training, I decided to create a series of bird illustrations. Working with Adobe Illustrator, I translated real-life reference photos into clean, vector-based art. A key part of the process was finding the right balance of detail; I intentionally simplified the forms to maintain a sleek look without losing the characteristic features of each species.

Kinetic typography

More information

This kinetic typography project was my first deep dive into Adobe After Effects. I focused on the graphical representation of lyrics, using creative associations to bring the song's message to life. A key element was the metaphorical use of imagery, such as trees representing different generations. Before starting, I ensured all copyright considerations were discussed with my instructor. This module was a turning point for me, as I realized how much I enjoy the dynamic world of motion graphics.

Fictional promotional video VJ Festival

More information

This project involved creating a teaser for a fictional 'Visual Arts Festival.' The challenge was to work with a standardized set of raw footage. To give my video a unique edge and a more professional feel, I supplemented the provided clips with high-quality, copyright-free stock footage. Since the festival concept centered on live video performances instead of traditional DJ sets, I focused on dynamic transitions and atmospheric visuals to convey the 'VJ-show' experience.

Film porject at school

More information

As part of our vocational training, I collaborated in a team of three to produce a short film from concept to final cut. Our project explored the theme of 'Freedom' and its diverse personal meanings. We intentionally chose a low-key, dark visual style, which presented a significant technical challenge: achieving clear footage in low light without excessive image noise from high ISO settings. While the filming was a collective effort, each team member produced an individual edit with a unique soundtrack to offer a personal interpretation of the footage.
